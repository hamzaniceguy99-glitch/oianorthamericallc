How we work

A Florida company, honest about the licensed parts

OIA North America LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations. It supplies trade customers, not consumers.

We are not a licensed customs broker and we file no customs entries; those are made by licensed brokers working with the importer of record. Product certification, labelling and regulatory approval remain the manufacturer's responsibility, supported by the right professionals — we'll flag the questions early rather than discover them at a port.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Do you buy stock or sell on commission?

Both arrangements exist in this trade. We'll discuss which fits your product and volumes, and whichever it is goes in writing before launch.

Will you take exclusivity immediately?

No, and you shouldn't want us to. Exclusivity makes sense once both sides have seen real demand, with performance terms attached.

Are you a customs broker?

No. We prepare and check documentation and work with your licensed broker. Entries are filed by licensed professionals.

Who handles product certification?

The manufacturer, with appropriate professional advice. We'll raise the questions early, and we don't advise on them ourselves.

Do you sell to consumers?

No. We supply trade customers — distributors, retailers and commercial buyers.

How do you report back?

Sales, stock levels and customer feedback on an agreed schedule, so the principal knows what's actually happening in the market.

Bringing a product to North America?

A free first conversation and an honest assessment of whether it fits this market.
