How we work

Presence, stock, paperwork

Buyers in this market treat a distant supplier as a risk. A local company, a local number and someone who answers in the same time zone removes most of that objection before price is even discussed.

Stock does the rest. Holding inventory here converts a quoted lead time of weeks into days, which is frequently the whole reason an account is won or lost.

And documentation decides whether goods move. Files that arrive incomplete sit at a port accumulating charges, so the paperwork is prepared and cross-checked before anything ships.
